## 📈 Key Business Insights
1. **Peak Demand Hours:** Order volume surges between **12:00 PM – 1:30 PM** (Lunch rush) and **5:00 PM – 7:30 PM** (Dinner peak).
2. **Category Performance:** Classic category drives the highest sales volume, while Supreme category yields higher margin contribution.
3. **Size Distribution:** Large (L) size pizzas contribute over 45% of total sales revenue.
